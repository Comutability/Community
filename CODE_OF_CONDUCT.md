# Community code of conduct

**Status: Proposed policy, pending community adoption.**

We want Comutability to be welcoming for people of different abilities, experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behaviour

Treat people respectfully. Offer constructive feedback, explain technical terms when helpful, and make room for newcomers. Challenge ideas without attacking people. Respect privacy and credit others' contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate exposure of private information and persistent abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until a private reporting channel and at least one trusted human contact are designated, do not publish sensitive complaints or personal details in a public issue. The founding coordinator must establish a confidential reporting channel and an impartial review/appeal process before this code is formally adopted.

Reports should be handled as confidentially as possible, with proportionate action and safeguards against conflicts of interest.
