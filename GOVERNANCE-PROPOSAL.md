# Governance proposal

**Status: Draft for discussion. Not yet ratified.**

## Roles

- **Founding coordinator:** coordinates the initial roadmap, access, infrastructure and decision log; initially the founder.
- **Maintainers:** review and merge changes to specific projects, oversee testing and handle releases under published policies.
- **Contributors:** suggest ideas, report defects, review, design, write, test, translate and code.
- **Community participants:** may take part in public discussions without being repository maintainers.

Roles are responsibilities, not ownership of contributors' work. No one is guaranteed a role merely by creating a pull request.

## Decisions

1. Create a public issue or discussion with the problem, alternatives, trade-offs and proposal.
2. Invite feedback, including accessibility, privacy, security and maintenance concerns.
3. The relevant maintainer, or the founding coordinator while no maintainers are appointed, records a decision and reasons.
4. Substantial changes to community-wide rules should receive a clearly announced public consultation before adoption.
5. Record decisions in the `decisions/` folder. Publish reversals and amendments, too.

The community may adopt broader consensus or voting mechanisms as participation grows, but no voting mechanism is established by this draft.

## Safeguards

- Respectful behaviour is required.
- Credentials, security keys and private user information must never be published in repositories.
- No self-approval of security-sensitive changes without a documented exception and independent verification.
- Prefer pull requests and keep the default branch protected once appropriate controls are available.
- Disclose conflicts of interest affecting proposals, sponsorship or procurement.
- Public decisions may omit security-sensitive details to prevent exploitation or privacy harm; explain that a restricted review occurred.

## Open governance questions

- How are future maintainers selected, replaced and removed?
- What qualifies as consensus?
- How are disputes or appeals resolved?
- What permissions can AI agents hold?
- What form of fiscal sponsorship or donation handling is appropriate, if any?
- What copyright and contribution approach should each project use?
