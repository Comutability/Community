# Decision 0001: Adopt Comutability Founding Charter v1.1

**Date:** 28 October 2026  
**Status:** Approved by founder  
**Scope:** Initial community identity and mission

## Decision

Adopt *Comutability* (Community + Usability) as the provisional name for the independent, community-led, AI-assisted open-source software initiative.

Approve the mission, vision, ten principles and founding promise in [Founding Charter v1.1](../FOUNDING-CHARTER.md).

**Founding promise:** Built together. Open to everyone.

## Boundaries

This approval is not the formation of a commercial company, the adoption of a legal entity, an approved governance mechanism, or a software release. It does not authorise any particular repository licence or AI release process.
