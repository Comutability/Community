# Financial Contribution Policy

**Organisation:** Comutability  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed for public review, not approved  
**Drafted:** 15 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Financial contributions made through Open Collective, when a fiscal host and payment arrangements have been approved

> This document is a proposal, not an adopted policy. The Founding Charter v1.1 remains the only approved governance document. No financial contribution facility should be represented as operational until its legal recipient, fiscal host, fees, terms and human approval have been confirmed.

## 1. Our purpose

Comutability is an independent, community-led, AI-assisted open-source initiative. Financial contributions help us build, test, document, secure and maintain genuinely useful and accessible open-source technology. Contributions are voluntary support for the mission, not investments, purchases of software ownership or payments for decision-making authority.

## 2. Contributions we welcome

We welcome one-off and recurring contributions from individuals, communities, non-profit organisations and businesses whose support is lawful and compatible with our mission, independence and Founding Charter.

We may decline, reject, return or stop accepting contributions where required by law or our eventual fiscal host, or where there are reasonable concerns about conflicts of interest, misleading sponsorship claims or conditions that undermine our principles. We will endeavour to explain a rejection appropriately, without disclosing confidential information.

We will not accept contributions conditional on:
- Control over Comutability, Secure Net, any application, governance, project priorities or technical standards.
- Votes, maintainer appointments, privileged access, security exceptions or influence over releases.
- Exclusive ownership of community work, restrictions inconsistent with the applicable open-source licence or a requirement to weaken privacy, security or accessibility.
- Concealing material terms of a sponsorship or falsely suggesting that Comutability endorses the contributor's products or views.

An organisation's contribution does not signify endorsement by Comutability.

## 3. How money may be used

Subject to human approval and the fiscal host's requirements, contributions may support:
- Infrastructure, hosting, domains and essential services.
- Development, maintenance, build systems, automated tests and testing equipment.
- Independent security assessments and remediation.
- Accessibility testing, including with disabled users and assistive technologies.
- Documentation, translations, education and community support.
- Properly approved expenses, contracts and other work directly advancing Comutability's open-source mission.

Unless a contribution is explicitly accepted for a defined project or purpose, it supports Comutability's general mission. Restricted funds must be tracked against their stated purpose. If that purpose can no longer be met, any change, transfer or refund must follow the applicable contributor agreement and fiscal-host rules.

Contributing does not guarantee that any particular feature, project or release will be delivered.

## 4. Financial transparency and oversight

Contributions and approved expenditures will be recorded through the applicable Open Collective account and fiscal host, with the financial transparency provided by that service. We will explain the identity and jurisdiction of the legal recipient, material platform and processing fees, and the permitted use of funds before collecting money.

Funds may be spent only for legitimate, documented work consistent with Comutability's mission and the fiscal host's requirements. Spending and reimbursements require appropriate human authorisation; an AI agent cannot independently approve or spend funds.

We will publish plain-language updates on funded work where practicable, without exposing sensitive personal or security information.

## 5. Supporter recognition and privacy

Supporters may choose the public attribution or anonymity options available through Open Collective. Contributions and transactions may appear in its public financial records, even when a contributor's identity is hidden.

We will not separately publish a supporter's personal name, logo or promotional endorsement without permission. We will only request personal information required for lawful payment processing, receipts and fiscal-host compliance, and will not sell supporter information.

## 6. Recurring contributions and refunds

A recurring supporter may change or cancel future contributions through their Open Collective account. Cancellation does not automatically refund completed payments.

Refunds are considered in accordance with Open Collective's terms, the fiscal host's rules, payment-processor limitations and applicable law. Contributors should contact Comutability or the fiscal host with the transaction date, amount and relevant transaction details. We will not promise a refund that the host or payment provider cannot authorise.

## 7. Taxes, fees and contributor expectations

Comutability is not currently registered as a charity or separate legal entity. A contribution must not be described as tax-deductible unless the actual fiscal host and applicable law establish that status.

A contribution is not an investment and gives no equity, profit share, preferential service, voting entitlement or governance authority. Any platform tips, payment fees and fiscal-host charges are separate matters governed by the relevant providers and must be disclosed where applicable.

## 8. Review, governance and contact

This policy is subject to Founding Charter v1.1. It will become effective only following a recorded decision by an authorised human steward under Comutability's current governance arrangements. Future material changes should be published for comment and recorded transparently.

Questions about contributions, restrictions or refunds may be sent to **contact@comutability.org**. Please do not send payment card details or other sensitive financial information by email.
