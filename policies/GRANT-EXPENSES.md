# Grant Expenses Policy

**Organisation:** Comutability  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposal pending human approval  
**Drafted:** 15 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Comutability's Open Collective finances, once a fiscal host and authorised reviewers are confirmed

> **Not yet adopted.** This policy is a proposal, subordinate to the approved Founding Charter v1.1 and to applicable law, Open Collective terms and fiscal-host requirements.

**Built together. Open to everyone.**

## Scope

This policy applies to **grants Comutability may award**, not grants it may receive. It does **not** establish an active grant programme or promise available funding. Grant expenses must remain disabled until the programme, budget, approval route and fiscal host have been authorised.

## What a grant may support

Proposed awards may fund useful, accessible and freely licensed open-source development, Secure Net protocol research or security review, accessibility work, documentation, translations, education, community activities, and essential public-interest infrastructure that advances Comutability's mission.

## Who may apply

Contributors, researchers, developers, designers, educators and eligible organisations may apply regardless of professional status. Every recipient must satisfy applicable payment, identity and fiscal-host requirements.

## Proposal requirements

Discuss the proposal with Comutability before submitting a grant expense. Provide the project purpose, public benefit, deliverables, proposed licence where relevant, itemised budget and currency, schedule and milestones, funding already secured, maintenance approach, and a suitable link to supporting work or repository. Avoid publishing confidential personal or security information.

## Assessment and human approval

Human reviewers assess alignment with Founding Charter v1.1, accessibility, practical impact, open-source commitments, security, feasibility, costs, available funds, sustainability and any conflicts of interest. AI may help with research and drafting but cannot approve awards. An applicant or conflicted reviewer must not decide their own grant. Funding may be divided into agreed milestones and remains subject to fiscal-host approval.

## Reporting and permitted changes

Recipients may be required to report progress, publish agreed deliverables under recognised open-source licences, provide evidence of funded work, and submit a final use-of-funds account. Material changes in purpose, scope or spending need written human approval first. Fraud, misleading claims or non-compliance may justify withholding future payments or other remedies under the award agreement and applicable law.

## Ineligible work and transparency

No awards for unlawful activity, misleading or duplicate funding, work that undermines privacy or security, unrelated personal benefit, hidden proprietary restrictions that contradict the grant agreement, or buying governance votes, maintainer rights, exclusivity or standards control. Grant awards and payments will be reported openly within platform and legal privacy limits.

An applicant may request an explanation or reconsideration by an appropriate independent human reviewer, where available.

**This policy becomes effective only after a recorded decision and a separately approved grant programme.**
