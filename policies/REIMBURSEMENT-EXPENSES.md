# Reimbursement Expenses Policy

**Organisation:** Comutability  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposal pending human approval  
**Drafted:** 15 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Comutability's Open Collective finances, once a fiscal host and authorised reviewers are confirmed

> **Not yet adopted.** This policy is a proposal, subordinate to the approved Founding Charter v1.1 and to applicable law, Open Collective terms and fiscal-host requirements.

**Built together. Open to everyone.**

## Purpose

Reimbursements repay contributors for **approved project purchases they have already paid for**. They do not replace invoices for paid labour or services.

## Eligible expenditure

Reasonable, necessary, project-related purchases may include hosting and domains, storage and infrastructure, development or testing tools, security and accessibility testing resources, documentation materials, approved equipment, approved community workshops, and necessary pre-approved travel or accommodation.

## Permission before spending

Before buying anything you expect the community to repay, seek prior written approval from an authorised human maintainer or financial administrator. Approval should state the project, purpose, expected cost, currency and spending ceiling. Unauthorised purchases are not guaranteed reimbursement. AI agents may assist with checking receipts, but have no spending or approval authority.

## How to claim

Use the **Reimbursement** expense type on Comutability's Open Collective page. Include a clear title, project, purchase date, original currency and amount, purpose, prior authorisation reference and an itemised receipt or proof of purchase. Explain any currency conversion. Where only part of a receipt is claimed, identify the relevant lines.

Claims must be submitted by the person or organisation that actually paid, and must not duplicate a claim to Comutability or another funder. Submit banking details only through the platform's secure process; do not expose private details in public transaction descriptions.

## Not covered

Personal or unrelated expenditure, duplicate claims, fines, penalties, unlawful items, unreasonably expensive purchases, unnecessary upgrades and purchases conflicting with the Charter or the fiscal host's rules will not ordinarily be paid. Any exception needs documented independent human approval and host acceptance.

## Review and payment

An authorised human reviewer verifies the approval, itemised receipt, project benefit and available budget. The claimant cannot approve their own request. The fiscal host also reviews its compliance requirements. Payment timing is determined by the provider and host; submission does not guarantee payment.

Reimbursement titles, descriptions and amounts may be visible publicly. Claimants can ask for reasons for a declined claim or seek independent review where available; personal details should be handled privately.

**This policy becomes effective only after recorded human approval.**
